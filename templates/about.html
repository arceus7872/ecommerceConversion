ECOMMERCE CONVERSION ANALYSIS

About the Project

Machine learning powered visitor conversion analysis designed to turn browsing behavior into actionable insights.

01

Problem Statement

Understanding whether a visitor is likely to purchase before the session ends.

This project predicts whether an online shopping session is likely to result in a purchase based on visitor behavior and session-level attributes.

Instead of simply providing a binary prediction, the application estimates the visitor's purchase probability, analyzes behavioral signals, explains the prediction, and suggests possible business actions.

02

Machine Learning Approach

The prediction pipeline combines preprocessing, classification, and explainable AI.

01

Data Preparation

The dataset is cleaned and duplicate records are removed before model training.

02

Feature Processing

Numerical features are scaled and categorical variables are converted using one-hot encoding.

03

Random Forest

A Random Forest classifier is used to estimate the probability of a visitor making a purchase.

04

SHAP Explanation

SHAP identifies the features that contributed positively or negatively to each prediction.

03

Why SHAP?

Making individual machine learning predictions easier to understand.

✦

From prediction to explanation

A purchase probability alone does not explain why the model reached its conclusion. SHAP helps identify which individual features pushed the prediction toward or away from the purchase class.

04

Real-World Use

Turning prediction results into potential ecommerce actions.

◈

Personalization

Prioritize personalized experiences for visitors showing stronger purchase signals.

◈

Product Recommendations

Recommend relevant products based on the visitor's browsing behavior.

◈

Engagement Strategy

Identify sessions where engagement could be improved before applying aggressive offers.

◈

Conversion Actions

Use behavioral insights to identify opportunities for reducing friction and improving conversion.

Built with Python, Flask, Scikit-learn and SHAP.
